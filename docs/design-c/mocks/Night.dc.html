2 Wave field

A stretched membrane carries a discrete set of transverse modes. We map the field at fixed tension for three drive frequencies [4].

2.1 Modal response

For a square membrane of side L, the normal modes separate along each axis:

fmn = (c / 2L) √(m² + n²)

The nodal lines divide the surface into regions of alternating phase (Figure 2). Higher modes split the plate into a checkerboard…
