MEMBRANE ATLASFIELD NOTES
2 Wave field

A stretched membrane supports a discrete set of transverse modes. We measure the field at fixed tension across three drive frequencies [4].

2.1 Modal response

For a square membrane of side L, the normal modes separate along each axis:

fmn = (c / 2L) √(m² + n²)(3)

The nodal lines divide the surface into regions of alternating phase, as shown in Figure 2. Higher modes split the plate into a checkerboard, so the (2, 2) response nearly vanishes at the centre.

(a) (1, 1)
(b) (2, 1)
(c) (2, 2)

Figure 2. Transverse displacement fields.

2.2 Boundary conditions

At the clamped edge the displacement vanishes, and the slope is free only along the diagonal supports.
